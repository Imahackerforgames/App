Sizing

Loc length chart

Not sure what length to book under? Let your locs hang loose and see where they fall.

  • Shoulder — locs rest at the top of your shoulders
  • Midback — roughly level with your bra line
  • Waist — the narrowest part of your torso
  • Butt — locs reach the bottom of your seat
  • Thigh — past your seat, down the thigh
Policies

Before booking please be sure to thoroughly read all policies.

Good to know

  • All washes are complimentary with your service.
  • No extra guests are allowed.
  • Snacks and drinks are provided at your appointment.
  • Bookings open on the 27th of each month.
  • Your deposit is half of your total service and is non-refundable. No refunds are given under ANY circumstances.

What I don’t offer

  • Loc extensions
  • Loc combinations
  • Reattachments exceeding 10 locs
  • Wick maintenance
  • MicroLoc services

Late fees, cancellations & more

  • 10–15 minutes late results in a $20 fee applied to your remaining balance at the end of your appointment.
  • 20 minutes late and your appointment is cancelled — your card is charged 100% of the service total.
  • Failing to show up without prior notification results in 100% of the service fee charged to the card on file.
  • Any cancellation or reschedule must be made 48 hours prior to your appointment.
  • Any locked cards or insufficient funds will be banned from booking.
