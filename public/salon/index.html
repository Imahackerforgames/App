Read before you book

The rules

None of these are new and none of them bend. They’re what keeps every appointment on time.

$10 deposit

Non-refundable, sent to $thehairrookiee immediately after you book. No deposit, no appointment — it gets cancelled.

No extra guests

Absolutely none, no matter the circumstance. Showing up with someone cancels the appointment on the spot.

Address after deposit

The studio location is texted to you the moment the deposit comes through. It isn’t posted publicly.

48-hour changes

Cancel or reschedule any time up to 48 hours before your appointment. Inside 48 hours the deposit is gone.

15-minute grace

You get 15 minutes. After that the appointment is cancelled and the deposit stays with the slot you held.

Business hours

Sunday 10AM–8PM. Monday through Thursday 4PM–9PM. Friday and Saturday are closed.
